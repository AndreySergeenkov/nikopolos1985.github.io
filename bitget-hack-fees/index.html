Who Earned Fees From the Laundering of Bitget’s Stolen Funds

By Andrey Sergeenkov · October 2, 2026
Key Findings

Laundering Bitget’s stolen funds generated fees:

  • $761,725 for protocols and services.
  • $259,718 for THORChain affiliates with additional financial links to the hackers’ wallets.
  • $206,196 for affiliates with no such links established yet.

On September 24, 2026, funds were stolen from Bitget’s hot and warm wallets. Laundering the stolen funds generated fees for the protocols and services used to move and exchange them. The chart below shows which protocols and services benefited and how much they earned in fees.

Fee Recipients Linked to the Bitget Hackers’ Wallets

THORChain swaps can include a separate affiliate fee. The parties moving Bitget’s stolen funds signed swap instructions naming the fee recipients. Those recipient addresses provide a starting point for tracing financial links to the hackers’ wallets. An interface or script can insert the recipient before the sender signs, for example to collect a service’s referral fee. Naming a recipient therefore does not, by itself, establish who controls that address.

The reviewed transactions document additional financial links for the recipients below. Some recipients returned fee proceeds to a wallet that submitted a stolen-fund swap or transferred funds to another linked fee recipient. In other cases, swaps naming different fee recipients sent the main stolen funds to the same Bitcoin destination wallets. The “Evidence” entries show the transactions supporting each link.

A closer look at thor18dvgrgpvxlh7rhhld4qjyrxs9c7tvwdakrkjm4 shows where some of its fees went. It exchanged part of those fees, paid in RUNE, for USDT. Those proceeds passed through two Ethereum wallets before reaching an address labeled OKX Hot Wallet 5 by Etherscan. OKX could check its internal deposit records to investigate whether these transfers are linked to a customer account and who holds it.

    Method and sources

    Figures cover the tracked transaction sample, use historical USD prices and are updated manually. Dates are UTC; displayed amounts are rounded to dollars. CSV and JSON retain cents.

    • THORChain: liquidity and affiliate fees recorded in Midgard swaps.
    • MetaMask: fee transfers to the identified recipient on Ethereum.
    • Chainflip: broker fees on matched swap requests.
    • CoW EthFlow: executed protocol and partner fees on settled trades.

    Separate BSC fees are outside this calculation. Individual shares of THORChain liquidity and CoW fees have not been calculated. Holding-account amounts are credits; final payouts have not been traced.

    These figures measure recorded fees in the tracked sample, not net profits or a complete accounting of every stolen-fund route. The article preparation date, the data cutoff and the dates of evidence reviews describe different events.

    Receiving an affiliate fee does not establish common ownership. Reviewed connections, supporting transactions and review dates appear under “Evidence”.
